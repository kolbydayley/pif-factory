## Review quality concerns requiring resolution

- Changelog e08/e09 remain `candidate` after final review despite describing
  community enrollment and the show's weekly live schedule. The reviewer expanded
  their evidence but did not address promotional/operational contamination.
  Exact quotation alone does not make these strategic intelligence.
- All-In e01: original supportive stance on factory farming was wrong, but the
  review's neutral replacement also discards the explicit negative attitude
  (the speaker calls it horrible). The final gate itself needs a source-based
  challenge, not automatic adoption of its correction.
- Some corrections are copy editing rather than material errors: singular
  article versus plural articles, or enter versus land in. These must not inflate
  critical-event error rates or force a verbatim-only claim paraphrase contract.
- The Gucci correction introduces a broader-business antecedent that is not
  established by the inspected local passage. Full-window context is needed;
  the correction must not silently substitute one uncertain antecedent for another.
- Old 477 candidates versus new 198 is a count difference, not a recall result.
  Every omission family needs source adjudication before interpreting the reduction.
